🛡️ What is Phishing?

Phishing is a cyber attack where criminals impersonate trusted organizations (like banks, Google, or Netflix) using fake emails, messages, or websites to steal your passwords, banking details, or OTPs.

📧 Email Phishing

Mass deceptive emails sent to thousands to steal login passwords or bank cards.

🎯 Spear Phishing

Personalized attacks tailored specifically against an individual or company employee.

📱 Smishing & Vishing

Phishing attempts delivered through SMS messages (Smishing) or voice calls (Vishing).

🔍 How to Identify Phishing Attacks

1

Check Sender Address

Look at the actual email address behind the display name (e.g. alerts@bob-kyc-alert.com).

2

Artificial Urgency

Be suspicious when an email demands urgent action within 10 minutes or 24 hours.

3

Hover Before Clicking

Hover your mouse cursor over any link to preview where it really redirects.

4

Never Disclose OTPs

Legitimate organizations and banks will never ask for your password or OTP.

5

Look for Typos

Watch for misspelled bank domains such as bankofbar0da.com or bob-kyc-update.net.

6

Unexpected Attachments

Never download or open unexpected file attachments ending in .exe, .scr, or .zip.

⚠️ Real-Life Phishing Example

Look at this simulated email. Click the button to inspect the red flags!

From: Bank of Baroda Alerts <alerts@bob-kyc-verification.net>

To: customer@example.com

Subject: URGENT: Your BOB NetBanking & Debit Card will be blocked in 24 hours!

Dear Bank of Baroda Customer,

Your Bank of Baroda (BOB) NetBanking KYC is expired. As per RBI guidelines, all banking transactions and ATM debit card services will be blocked within 24 hours.

Please update your PAN Card and Aadhaar details immediately to restore your account services.

Destination: http://192.168.1.5/fake-bob-netbanking-login.php

Simulated training scenario. Never enter real banking credentials.

🚩 Red Flags Found in this Email:

  • Fake Bank Domain: Notice bob-kyc-verification.net is a fake external domain. Official Bank of Baroda emails end in bankofbaroda.in.
  • Urgency & Fear: Threatening "NetBanking & Debit Card Blocked in 24 hours" creates panic so you act quickly without thinking.
  • Generic Greeting: Uses "Dear Bank of Baroda Customer" instead of your real name or masked account number.
  • Insecure Link: The button directs to an unencrypted IP address (http://192.168...) rather than the official HTTPS Bank of Baroda portal.

🧠 Social Engineering Techniques

😱 Fear

Threatening account closure, police fines, or legal action to make you act without thinking.

⏰ Urgency

Creating fake countdowns or limited time windows to prevent you from checking facts.

🎭 Impersonation

Pretending to be a company CEO, IT manager, bank officer, or government agency.

🎁 Reward

Offering fake cash prizes, free iPhones, or lottery winnings to lure you into sharing info.
